After completing the meaningful **First Cloud AI Journey** internship program, I would like to share my personal thoughts and feedback below in the hope of contributing to the improvement of the program for future participants:

### Overall Evaluation of the Program

**1. General Overview**

First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) provides a well-structured learning path that combines foundational knowledge, hands-on practice with AWS, and professional knowledge-sharing activities. Throughout the program, I had the opportunity to strengthen my understanding of Cloud Computing while gaining a clearer perspective on how AWS services can be used and integrated to solve real-world problems.

**2. Learning Environment and Community Activities**

The learning environment is open and encourages participants to actively exchange knowledge and experiences. Discussions and knowledge-sharing activities provided me with different perspectives when dealing with challenges during hands-on practice. In addition to technical knowledge, participating in community activities also helped me improve my communication skills and ability to share knowledge with others.

**3. Support from Mentors**

Mentors played an important role throughout my learning journey, especially when I encountered difficulties during hands-on exercises. Instead of solving problems directly for participants, mentors often provided guidance and suggestions that encouraged us to identify the root cause and find appropriate solutions ourselves. This approach helped me develop the habit of actively researching documentation, checking logs, and analyzing problems before deciding on a solution.

**4. Learning Path and Experience**

The program's learning path is structured from basic to more advanced topics. I was introduced to areas including IAM, EC2, S3, VPC, databases, Serverless, containers, monitoring, and automation. Combining theoretical learning with hands-on practice helped me better understand how different AWS services work together rather than studying each service independently.

**5. Knowledge and Skills Gained**

After completing the program, I found that I had made progress in several areas:

- **Cloud Knowledge:** Gained a better understanding of AWS architecture and the use of common AWS services.
- **Practical Skills:** Gained hands-on experience deploying and configuring AWS resources through the AWS Management Console.
- **Troubleshooting Skills:** Learned how to check service status, analyze logs, and identify the causes of system issues.
- **Automation:** Gained initial experience using scripts and supporting tools to automate tasks on AWS.
- **System Thinking:** Developed a better understanding of how multiple AWS services can be combined to build a complete system.
- **Documentation and Reporting:** Improved my ability to document the learning process, create worklogs, and present the knowledge I acquired.

---

### Suggestions for Program Development

In addition to the existing content, I believe the program could be further improved in the following areas:

- **Increase the Number of Hands-on Exercises:** Add more real-world scenarios so that participants have more opportunities to apply their knowledge in practice.

- **Expand CI/CD and IaC Content:** Allocate more time to topics such as Terraform, AWS CDK, CloudFormation, and CI/CD pipeline development.

- **Strengthen Troubleshooting Practice:** Introduce simulated scenarios such as connection errors, permission issues, system overload, or configuration mistakes, allowing participants to identify and resolve the problems themselves.

- **Add More AI-related Content:** Include topics related to Generative AI, Amazon Bedrock, and AI Agents to keep the program aligned with the latest developments in technology.

- **Increase Project Presentation Activities:** Organize more Demo/Presentation sessions where participants can introduce their projects, exchange experiences, and receive feedback from mentors.

---

### Final Thoughts

Throughout my time in the **First Cloud AI Journey** program, I had the opportunity to strengthen my AWS knowledge and gain hands-on experience with various Cloud services. The labs, workshops, and support from mentors helped me better understand how theoretical knowledge can be applied to real-world situations.

I consider this program a valuable experience for both my learning process and career orientation. It has provided me with a stronger foundation to continue exploring **Cloud Computing, Cloud Security, and AI technologies**, while also helping me develop the habit of self-learning and taking a proactive approach to problem-solving in a professional environment.
